Send a Mapping Reference to GHL

Click the button below once. It will POST a sample payload (with every field both of your landing-page forms can send) to your GoHighLevel Inbound Webhook. GHL will capture it as a Mapping Reference template you can select in the workflow trigger.

After you click:
  1. Go back to the GHL workflow tab.
  2. In the Mapping Reference dropdown, click "Check for new requests".
  3. A new entry should appear — select it.
  4. Click Save Trigger, then toggle the workflow to Published.
