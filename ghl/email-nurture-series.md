**Subject:** Why cravings aren't a willpower problem
**Preview:** The blood sugar roller coaster, in plain English.

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

If you've ever thought "I just need more willpower," I want to gently push back on that.

When we eat, our blood sugar rises and our body releases insulin to bring it back down. When meals are heavy in fast carbs, that rise can be quick and steep, and the drop afterward is what often brings the 3 p.m. crash, the brain fog, and the "I need something sweet" feeling.

Over time, some people's bodies stop responding to insulin as well as they used to. That's called insulin resistance, and it's far more common than most people realize.

The good news is that small, steady changes in how and when we eat can make a real difference in how we feel. That's what got my family and me looking at this differently.
